Pool Position

Keep score of every pool set with your rival.

Pool Position is a scorebook for two players who play 8-ball and 9-ball together. Score best-of-three sets rack by rack, see who's on the break next time, track solids and stripes, record wild moments, and keep a record of every hall you play at.

Each book is shared between the two of you through iCloud, so both phones see every rack as it happens. There's a Lock Screen Live Activity during a set, Home Screen widgets, and a layout made for iPhone Duo.
